# Passport Bro News Uploader: Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Passport Bro News Uploader ("the tool") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Passport Bro News
YouTube channel to upload and schedule videos to that channel.

## What data it accesses
With the channel owner's permission, given through Google OAuth, the tool:
- uploads videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's own channel and adds them to the owner's playlists;
- reads the channel's name and ID, to confirm the right account is signed in.

It does not access any other user's data.

## Where data is stored
The sign-in token and the IDs of uploaded videos are stored only on the owner's own computer.
Nothing is stored on any server operated for the tool.

## Sharing
No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The tool has no users other than the channel owner.

## Revoking access
Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## YouTube and Google
This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service
(https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). See also the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
